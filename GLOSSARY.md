# Course Platform

An online course platform where learners buy courses and can later ask for their money back.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to have a Purchase refunded, carrying their stated reason. It is Pending until an Admin Approves or Declines it.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, open request

**Refund**:
The return of the amount the learner actually paid for a Purchase, made when their Refund Request is Approved. It ends the learner's access to the course. A Declined Refund Request produces no Refund.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days after a Purchase during which a learner may raise a Refund Request.

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a learner may raise a Refund Request for a Purchase. They may only if it is within the Refund Window, they have not watched too much of the course, the seat is not a claimed team seat, and no Refund Request is already Pending. A Declined Refund Request does not stop the learner raising another.

**Refunded Purchase**:
A Purchase whose Refund Request was Approved. It no longer gives the learner access to the course, but the learner's lesson progress is kept. If it bought team seats, unclaimed seats can no longer be claimed; learners who already claimed a seat keep their access. The learner may buy the course again as a new Purchase.

**Approve** / **Decline**:
The two decisions an Admin makes on a Pending Refund Request. A decision is final and cannot be undone. Approving records the Refund; it does not itself move money.
_Avoid_: Resolve

**Decline Reason**:
The Admin's explanation, written to the learner, of why their Refund Request was Declined. Every Decline has one.

**Chargeback**:
Reserved for a payment dispute a cardholder raises through their bank. It is not part of our refund process and is not currently modelled.
