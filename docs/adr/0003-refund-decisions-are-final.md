# Refund decisions are final

Once an Admin Approves or Declines a Refund Request the decision cannot be undone or edited, and deciding a request that is no longer Pending is refused. Approving has side effects that cannot be cleanly reversed (the learner is emailed, access ends, unclaimed team seat codes are voided), and the decision record is the audit trail for money leaving the business. A Declined learner who is still eligible can raise a new Refund Request instead of the old one being reopened.
