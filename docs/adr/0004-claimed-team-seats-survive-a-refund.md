# Claimed team seats survive a Refund

When a team Purchase is refunded, only its unclaimed seat codes are voided; learners who already claimed a seat keep their access, even though the buyer gets the full amount paid back. We chose not to punish team members for the buyer's refund. Normally a team Purchase with any claimed seat is not eligible for a refund at all, so this only arises when a seat is claimed after the Refund Request was raised and an Admin Approves anyway (they are warned).
